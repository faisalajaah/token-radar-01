Which pre-token protocol is worth farming?

radar-3 scores every pre-token protocol in a sealed DefiLlama snapshot (14 Nov 2026) by expected launch value — the FDV its TVL implies, times the chance it ever launches. The multiples are calibrated from the snapshot itself: each category's multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of its live-token protocols, times a 2.5x FDV uplift. No name is ever special-cased.

A protocol is listed as pre-token because its DefiLlama record carries no token — that is the record, not proof it has none.

What moves the number

  • Growth is spike-damped: a +300% week pays about as much as a +120% one, and a spike over 100% costs 5 points of launch chance as reversion risk.
  • Maturity peaks at one year and holds to 900 days, then decays — a protocol that ran four years with no token is worth less than the same one at eighteen months.
  • Chains pay diminishing returns: 1 + 0.06 × log₂(chains), capped at 1.20.
  • Launch chance starts in the age band (180–900 days scores 0.78) and moves with the group's token base rate, growth and chain count, clamped to 0.12–0.90.
